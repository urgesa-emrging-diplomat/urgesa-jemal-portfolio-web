ABOUT ME

From grassroots experience to global engagement.

My journey began in a rural community in West Arsi, Ethiopia, where I experienced firsthand the barriers young people can face when access to education, information, infrastructure and opportunity is limited.

Those experiences shaped my commitment to ensuring that young people, especially underrepresented youth, have meaningful opportunities to participate in leadership, policy and development.

Today, my work connects youth leadership, ethical governance, diplomacy, peacebuilding, civic participation and sustainable development. I believe young people should not only be consulted about the future. They should help shape it.
